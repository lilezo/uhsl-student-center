Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to policies, equipment checkout, and facility access below.

The Student Center is open Monday through Thursday from 7:30 AM to 9:30 PM, Friday from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. The facility is closed on Sundays and recognized University holidays.

Study rooms can be reserved up to 14 days in advance. Individual students or groups are limited to a maximum of 2 consecutive hours per day to ensure equitable access across all student groups.

Students with an active Cougar Card can check out HDMI/USB-C display adapters, wireless presenter remotes, USB conference microphones, webcams, and mobile dry-erase kit markers from the front desk free of charge.

Covered drinks and personal dry snacks are permitted in general study spaces. Food is strictly prohibited in the Esports and Digital Media Lounge. Registered student organizations hosting catered events must receive prior approval.

The Front Information Desk in the main atrium manages the campus lost and found service. High-value property like electronics or student IDs are transferred to UH Campus Police after 48 hours.
